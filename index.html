Research

Magnetics are ubiquitous in modern electronics: from inductors and transformers in switched power converters to magnetostatic wave filters. As semiconductor technologies continue to scale and heterogeneous integration advances, there is an increasing need to miniaturize magnetic components correspondingly and integrate them with other electronics at the chip or package level to form microsystems. Our research focuses on integrated magnetics and microsystems, leveraging microfabrication techniques to miniaturize magnetic components and advanced packaging technologies to build integrated microsystems that enable the seamless integration of magnetics with other electronic components.

Keywords: integrated magnetics, power magnetics, inductor, transformer, microfabrication, magnetic materials, and packaging.
